Project Brief: Luminous Beauty AR

Vision

A premium, AI-native AR Makeup Coaching social platform that empowers users to learn professional makeup techniques through real-time guidance rather than static filters. The platform bridges the gap between following a beauty influencer and mastering their techniques on one's own face.

Core Value Proposition





Expert Coaching: Step-by-step AR overlays provide anatomical guidance (ellipses for blush, band highlights for contour, etc.).



Real-time Feedback: Intelligent tracking provides non-intrusive feedback on placement and blending.



Creator Empowerment: Influencers can easily map and publish their signature "recipes" using intuitive creator tools.

Key Features

1. Discovery & Social (Home / Explore)





Visual Feed: High-quality look cards with difficulty levels and time estimates.



Filtering: Quick access to specific techniques (Blush, Lip, Contour) and difficulty tiers.



Social Integration: Creator profiles with statistics, featured masterclasses, and video recipe feeds.

2. AR Coaching Experience





Interactive Camera UI: Full-screen AR view with precise anatomical overlays.



Dynamic Guidance: A draggable bottom sheet providing specific instructions and product shade info.



Progress Tracking: Real-time coverage percentages and completion checklists.



Tracking Stability: Visual indicators for lighting and face tracking quality.

3. Review & Commerce





Before/After Comparison: Interactive slider to visualize the transformation.



Integrated Shopping: Direct links to products used in the recipe with options for alternatives.



Social Sharing: Achievement badges and "Save to My Looks" functionality.

4. Creator Studio





Recipe Editor: A timeline-based tool to segment makeup steps.



Mapping Tools: Visual tools (soft brush, ellipse, mirror) for creators to define AR coaching zones on a face frame.



Product Linking: Seamless integration of specific product metadata into AR steps.

Design Identity





Visual Style: Modern, premium, clean, and minimalist.



Color Palette: Soft neutrals (Luminous Beauty design system) with high-contrast typography and subtle depth.



UI Elements: Rounded corners (8px), gentle shadows, and glass-like overlays for AR components to maintain clarity over the camera feed.



Tone: Professional, coach-like, and authoritative yet accessible.

Technical Requirements





Device: Mobile (iOS/Android).



AR Tech: Real-time facial landmark tracking and region-based coverage analysis.



Accessibility: High-contrast text, legible font sizes (Inter), and generous touch targets.
